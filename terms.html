Usage terms

Ambient Music is a local tool for authorized channel owners and their operators.

Authorized use

Use the app only with channels you own or are authorized to manage. Review the destination channel, publishing settings, and content before authorizing an upload. The channel owner controls the app's publishing instructions and remains responsible for the content and rights required to publish it.

YouTube and Google requirements

Use must comply with YouTube's Terms of Service, its community guidelines, and the applicable Google and YouTube API policies. Google may limit quotas, change API behavior, expire authorization, or require additional verification.

Access and privacy

Read the privacy policy before connecting an account. You can stop using the app and revoke access through your Google Account. Keep the local authorization files accessible only to authorized operators.

Support

For questions, use the project issue tracker.
