LOCAL MUSIC PUBLISHING

Original music.
Owner-controlled publishing.

Ambient Music helps authorized channel owners prepare and upload original ambient and instrumental videos to YouTube.

What the app does

The uploader runs on the channel owner's computer. It uses the YouTube Data API to identify the authorized channel and upload the owner's video files with their titles, descriptions, and publishing settings.

The Autonomous Ambient project uses it for Sleep, Study, Meditation, and Chill.

Your authorization

Google's OAuth screen lets you grant upload permission and permission to view channel information. The app checks the channel identity before saving authorization.

Your control

Authorization is stored locally. You can revoke it through Google Account connections and remove the local authorization file.

Before connecting

Read our privacy policy for the data accessed, how it is used, and your controls. This website provides app information; it does not collect passwords or run the OAuth authorization process.

Questions? Contact the operator through the project issue tracker.
