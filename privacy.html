Privacy policy

This policy describes Ambient Music's local YouTube authorization and publishing tools.

Information the app accesses

With your Google OAuth consent, the app obtains an access token and refresh token. It uses YouTube upload permission to send video files and publishing metadata to your authorized channel. It uses YouTube read permission to retrieve the channel ID, name, handle, and upload eligibility, and may retrieve channel video metadata needed to organize publishing.

The app does not request your Google password. Google handles sign-in and consent. The app also processes the video files, titles, descriptions, and settings you provide for publishing.

How information is used

Google user data is used to verify the intended channel, maintain the authorized connection, and carry out the channel owner's publishing instructions. Authorized operators and tools assisting them may see channel details and upload metadata when needed to perform these tasks.

The app does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train general-purpose AI models. Ambient Music's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and protection

The local authorization tool stores OAuth credentials and tokens in the operating system's local application-data folder, outside the project repository. The uploader uses them to make requests to Google over HTTPS. Credentials and tokens are excluded from the project's Git storage and are not displayed by the authorization tool.

Video files and publishing metadata are stored in locations chosen by the operator. YouTube stores uploaded videos and their metadata under the channel's selected publishing settings. Protect access to the computer and any backups containing local authorization files.

Sharing

The app sends tokens, uploads, and API requests to Google and YouTube as needed for the authorized functions. It does not operate a separate server database of Google user data. If the owner chooses to share project files or involve an assistant, that sharing is controlled by the owner. This informational website receives no OAuth tokens.

Retention, revocation, and deletion

Local tokens remain until removed or replaced, and are usable only while Google considers the authorization valid. You can revoke the app at Google Account connections. You can then delete its local authorization file; on Windows the Autonomous Ambient tool uses %LOCALAPPDATA%\AutonomousAmbient\youtube-token.json.

Remove local content and metadata from the locations you selected. To remove videos already uploaded, use YouTube Studio. Revoking app access does not delete uploaded videos.

This website

These static pages do not add analytics, advertising, login forms, or tracking cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process standard request information under GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes and contact

This page will be updated if the app's data practices change. The effective date is shown above. Contact the operator through the project issue tracker. Please keep credentials and other private information out of public issues.
